The Real Reasons People Put It Off

Whatever's Been Keeping You Away, We've Heard It.

Many adults wish they had straightened their teeth years ago. Here's how we handle the four most common reasons people delay — so you can stop putting it off.

The worry
"I'm too old for braces or Invisalign."
What actually happens
You're never too old. Many of our patients are adults in their 30s, 40s, and 50s. Invisalign is virtually invisible — most coworkers, friends, and even family won't even notice you're in treatment.
The worry
"I'm going to be pressured into expensive treatment."
What actually happens
You get a written treatment plan with transparent pricing. No surprises, no upselling. Dr. Hou's philosophy is simple — every patient deserves a treatment plan as unique as their smile, and never one they don't need.
The worry
"I can't afford it — and orthodontics is so expensive."
What actually happens
0% in-house financing for every treatment plan. Most PPO insurance accepted. Free consultations so you'll know exactly what your investment looks like — before anything starts.
The worry
"My English isn't great — will I be understood?"
What actually happens
Dr. Hou and her team speak English, Spanish, Vietnamese, and Chinese. Great care starts with great communication — and you'll always feel heard, in any language.
Still not sure? Just ask.
Text us any question — no appointment needed. Real response, usually within an hour during business hours.

Frequently Asked

Questions We Hear Most

Don't see your question? Ask us directly — we'll respond within an hour during business hours.

What insurance do you accept?
We accept most major PPO insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Aetna, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, and United Healthcare. We also offer 0% in-house financing for patients without insurance. Call us to verify your specific plan — we'll do it in 60 seconds.
How much does orthodontic treatment cost?
Treatment is customized to each patient's needs. We offer FREE consultations where we'll provide a detailed treatment plan and transparent pricing — no surprises. 0% in-house financing is available for every plan, making your dream smile achievable.
Do you offer free consultations?
Yes. Every new patient receives a complimentary consultation including a smile evaluation, digital scan, and custom treatment plan. Call or text (714) 374-9766 to schedule. No cost, no commitment.
Is Dr. Hou accepting new patients?
Yes — we warmly welcome new patients of all ages, from kids to adults. Book online or call us directly. Your first visit includes a full evaluation, digital scan, and time with Dr. Hou to discuss your goals and treatment options.
At what age should my child see an orthodontist?
The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial evaluation by age 7. Early evaluation allows Dr. Hou to identify issues early and plan optimal timing for treatment. Most kids don't need treatment immediately, but early monitoring leads to better outcomes.
Does Invisalign work for adults?
Absolutely — many of our adult patients choose Invisalign for its discreet appearance. Dr. Hou has treated thousands of patients with Invisalign over 20+ years. You're monitored in-person at every visit, which is why the results are predictable and beautiful.
Do you speak languages other than English?
Yes. Dr. Hou and her team speak English, Spanish, Vietnamese, and Chinese. Great care starts with great communication — and you'll always feel heard, in any language.
What is digital scanning and why does it matter?
We use advanced digital scanning technology that eliminates messy impressions. It allows for more accurate, comfortable treatment planning and a smoother experience from start to finish. Fewer surprises, better outcomes.
Where are you located and is parking available?
We're at 15015 Goldenwest Street, Huntington Beach, CA 92647, with free on-site parking. Easy access from the 405 freeway and from Costa Mesa, Fountain Valley, Westminster, and surrounding areas.
What are your hours?
Monday–Friday, 10 AM to 6 PM. Saturdays and Sundays available by appointment. Evening appointments available — just ask when you book.
